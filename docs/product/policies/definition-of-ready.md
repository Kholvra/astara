# Astara — Definition of Ready

An item may move from `discovery` to `specified` or `ready` only when the applicable checks below have evidence in its item package.

- [ ] The concrete problem or opportunity is stated.
- [ ] The actor, consumer, or affected system is identified.
- [ ] The desired outcome is observable.
- [ ] In-scope and explicitly out-of-scope behavior are recorded.
- [ ] Constraints and important invariants are recorded.
- [ ] Main-path and relevant failure or boundary behavior have verifiable acceptance criteria.
- [ ] Ambiguous behavior is resolved or explicitly marked as a decision needed.
- [ ] Data, compatibility, migration, recovery, and external dependencies are recorded when applicable.
- [ ] The item is one coherent outcome or its split is documented.
- [ ] Metadata and links agree with [`backlog.md`](../backlog.md).
- [ ] Human approval exists for business scope and priority when those decisions affect delivery.

Polished prose alone is not evidence of readiness.
