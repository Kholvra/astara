# Astara — Definition of Done for Requirements

An item is complete in the requirements domain when:

- its approved requirement, scope, and constraints are recorded;
- acceptance criteria are observable and verifiable;
- unresolved decisions are visible and owned;
- the backlog row and item metadata agree;
- validation evidence or the next evidence-producing step is linked;
- the readiness verdict is recorded;
- the handoff boundary to engineering is explicit.

This policy does not mean the product feature itself has been implemented. Engineering work begins only after the item reaches the appropriate readiness verdict.
